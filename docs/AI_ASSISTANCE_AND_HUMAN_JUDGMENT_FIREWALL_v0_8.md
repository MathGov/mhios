# MHIOS AI Assistance and Human-Judgment Firewall v0.8

## Governing rule

AI assistance may propose, summarize, organize, compare, calculate, and challenge. It may not certify its own evidence, create authority, or silently become the accountable decision-maker.

## Required provenance

Each AI contribution records origin status, model or service, task, source objects, timestamp, output hash, reviewer, modifications, and final disposition.

## Review dispositions

- Accepted without modification.
- Accepted with modification.
- Rejected.
- Contested.
- Deferred pending evidence.

## Prohibited automatic actions

- stakeholder exclusion;
- final evidence-credibility classification;
- rights-floor approval;
- catastrophic-risk acceptance;
- physical-admissibility warrant;
- legal or democratic authority assignment;
- emergency authorization;
- final SGP authority or role classification;
- execution authorization.

## Interface requirements

AI-generated content is visibly labeled. Users can inspect source inputs and uncertainty. Human acceptance creates a ReviewerJudgment. The interface remains functional with AI disabled. Bulk acceptance of material AI fields is not the default and requires explicit review.

## Evaluation

Pilot studies should measure hallucinated sources, omitted counterevidence, inappropriate confidence, reviewer automation bias, acceptance without inspection, and differences between AI-assisted and no-AI runs.

## Independent-first-pass control

For gate-material fields, the default interface withholds the AI suggestion until the reviewer records an initial judgment and rationale. The system then reveals the suggestion, records agreement or divergence, and preserves both passes. Exceptions are logged.

## Rubber-stamping diagnostic

Measure acceptance without modification, source inspection, review time, and divergence. Near-universal acceptance triggers sampling and process review rather than an efficiency claim.

## AI-off sampling

Compare sampled tasks under AI-assisted and AI-disabled conditions after establishing a no-AI baseline. Report omissions, commission errors, state differences, confidence, and burden.
