## Measurement and pilot hardening

The burden protocol no longer treats the Rigorous-to-Hollow Effort Ratio (RHER) as the primary first-pilot success measure. It separates four non-confounded empirical questions:

- valid-run completion burden;
- hollow-pass rate;
- time to hollow detection; and
- false-positive challenge burden.

Hollow-run detection probability is defined over the union of automated and human detections, with automated, human, overlap, and legitimate-run false-positive rates reported separately. RHER is exploratory only for comparable completion states; the former universal `RHER <= 1.50` candidate target is withdrawn pending pilot evidence.

The 1-7 burden scales now have explicit endpoints and direction rules. No overall burden composite is treated as valid until its measurement behavior is tested.

The inter-rater protocol is preregistration-ready at the protocol level: exact build and case hashes, reviewer eligibility/conflicts, training/calibration separation, neutral primary packets, randomization/counterbalancing, preselected primary reliability coefficient, confidence intervals, missingness/attrition handling, consequential state flips, and revision rules are specified. Calibration cases are excluded from the primary analysis; case authors do not rate their own primary cases; and RG claim-authority status is analyzed separately from RF/NCRC, TRC and CSV gate states and from RLS/authority outcomes.
