# MHIOS Institutional Preconditions Profile v0.8

## Purpose

This profile states the organizational conditions required for MHIOS records to function as more than decorative compliance.

## Minimum conditions

1. Budgeted reviewer time.
2. Non-retaliation protection for good-faith challenge and escalation.
3. Independent approval for downward tier divergence where feasible.
4. Authority to delay, narrow, redesign, escalate, or refuse.
5. Protected stakeholder and dissent channels.
6. Periodic outcome audits comparing tier and override choices with observed outcomes.
7. Defined remediation for confirmed evidence suppression, authority misstatement, or unjustified tier downgrading.

## Status model

`PRESENT`, `PARTIAL`, `ABSENT`, `CONTESTED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_RATIONALE`.

An absent gate-material condition narrows the assurance claim and requires an `INSTITUTIONAL_PRECONDITIONS_INCOMPLETE` warning. Software conformance cannot compensate for organizational incentives that punish honest review.

## Pilot measures

Record reviewer time sufficiency, escalation consequences, reporting-line independence, challenge outcomes, tier-downgrade frequency, and whether dissenting reviewers experienced adverse consequences.
