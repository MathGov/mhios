# MHIOS v0.8 Self-Assessment and Claim Boundary

## Current disposition

- Artifact integrity: testable and verified by the package verifier.
- Internal structural consistency: partially tested.
- Permission and execution-authorization currency: structurally and temporally tested against supplied synthetic vectors; legal validity and real control effectiveness untested.
- Normative executable coverage: incomplete and reported quantitatively.
- Construct validity: untested.
- Burden reduction: untested.
- Inter-rater reliability: untested.
- Anti-theater precision and recall: untested.
- Operational superiority: untested.
- Core-inclusion readiness: not established.

## Material design hypothesis

A shared evidence graph, progressive disclosure, explicit provenance, bounded automation, and generated records can reduce avoidable cognitive and clerical burden without materially reducing assurance.

## Claim type and dependency position

This is a mixed design, human-factors, governance, and implementation hypothesis. It depends on institutional incentives, reviewer authority, training, domain evidence, usability, security, accessibility, and honest adoption.

## Evidence surface

The v0.8 package supplies internal documents, schemas, examples, structural checks, a mutation suite, a normative-coverage ledger, a versioned execution-mandate contract, and a clickable prototype. These establish artifact properties and rejection of selected synthetic defects only. They do not establish that an authority is legitimate, a legal interpretation is correct, a signature or trust service is secure, a physical warrant is true, or a control is effective. Human-factors literature supports caution about automation over-reliance, not the effectiveness of MHIOS itself.

## Alternative explanations

- Training or institutional reform may create most of the benefit.
- Added structure may increase ritual compliance.
- Progressive disclosure may hide material complexity.
- AI assistance may homogenize judgments or reduce vigilance.
- Anti-theater rules may displace visible theater into harder-to-detect forms.
- A simpler checklist may achieve equal assurance at lower burden.

## Falsification and revision triggers

Revise, narrow, or reject parts of MHIOS if pilots show high burden without assurance gains, low consequential reviewer agreement, hollow runs passing cheaply, high false reassurance, automation increasing omission or commission errors, low replay success, or objects that cannot be linked to prevented or detected material errors.

## Current claim action

Proceed only to a narrow no-AI vertical slice, comparative pilot, and independent reviewer study. Do not claim validation, operational effectiveness, or deployment assurance.
